# Crisis Singularity

## 1. Definition

A crisis singularity is a candidate regime in which degrees of freedom collapse,
transition deformation spikes, and shadow pressure is forced into realization.

## 2. Structural Role

It is a diagnostic concept, not a claim that the system can universally detect
all crises.

## 3. Observable Traces

- `D_t` collapse
- `K_t` spike
- forced realization pressure
- benchmark residual stress

## 4. Cases

- [2008 GFC](../cases/2008_GFC.md)
- [2020 Treasury basis](../cases/2020_treasury_basis.md)
- [2022 LDI](../cases/2022_LDI.md)

## 5. Rejection Gate

If joint-hitting conditions do not improve diagnostic interpretation over broad
benchmarks, the singularity claim remains architectural rather than empirical.
