# AQR

## 1. Institution Type

Quantitative asset manager and public research producer.

## 2. Why It Matters

AQR materials are useful for factor, trend, carry, and institutional risk
language, especially when distinguishing strategy context from formal proxy data.

## 3. Materials Collected

- public research papers
- public commentaries
- data-library documentation

## 4. Usage

- institutional framing
- literature context
- benchmark or dataset reference only when separately registered

## 5. Not Allowed

- Do not use PDF-extracted numbers as formal proxy data.
- Do not treat single-manager framing as market-wide evidence.
