# 2023 SVB - Accounting Anchor Mismatch And Funding Path Contraction

## 1. Event Summary

SVB failed in March 2023 after duration losses, concentrated uninsured deposits,
and rapid deposit flight forced realization pressure.

## 2. Structural Diagnosis

- `M`: high
- `D`: collapsing
- `K`: moderate/high
- `X`: high latent duration pressure

## 3. Anchor Mismatch

Book-value/accounting anchors diverged from market-value/fair-value anchors.

## 4. Path Feasibility

Deposit flight reduced funding paths; asset sales crystallized losses.

## 5. Shadow Accumulation

Duration mismatch and HTM unrealized losses accumulated before realization.

## 6. Trigger Vs Potential

- Trigger: deposit run.
- Stored potential: duration losses, uninsured deposit concentration, accounting anchor gap.
