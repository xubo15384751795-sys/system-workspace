# Federal Reserve

## 1. Institution Type

US central bank, official data provider, policy actor, and research source.

## 2. Why It Matters

Fed data and policy actions are relevant for funding anchors, facilities,
balance-sheet flows, NFCI benchmarks, and crisis intervention paths.

## 3. Materials Collected

- FRED series
- H.4.1 releases
- speeches and policy documents
- research papers

## 4. Usage

- official data when manifest exists
- policy-path context
- benchmark/control for NFCI-related series

## 5. Not Allowed

- Do not treat Fed research prose as proxy data.
- Do not let NFCI enter `Sigma_t` core construction by default.
