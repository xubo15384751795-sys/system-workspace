# Rejected Or Weakened Claims

## Rejected

- `C003`: `Sigma_t` outperforms NFCI globally.

## Weakened

- `C004`: `K_proxy` measures true geometric curvature.

## Required Language

Use "diagnostic proxy", "candidate morphology", or "structural trace" when the
measurement is indirect.

Do not use "proves", "dominates", "validated", or "true curvature" without the
required claim maturity and evidence checks.

## Claim Discipline

Use the [Claim Registry](claim_registry.md) before paper-facing use.
