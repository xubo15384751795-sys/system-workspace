# Neutral Macro Pressure Measurement Cards

These gauges are observations, not a market theory and not a timing forecast.
They are deliberately independent of the archived Deformation v1 claim set.

## Funding mismatch pressure (`M` compatibility key)

- Product name: **Funding mismatch pressure**.
- Observable: standardized tension between short-term private funding costs,
  policy-administered rates, and broad credit conditions.
- Current evidence basket: CP-Treasury-bill spread, SOFR-IORB spread, and the
  NFCI credit sub-index.
- Mechanism lineage: funding liquidity and market liquidity can reinforce one
  another under intermediary balance-sheet constraints; see Brunnermeier and
  Pedersen, *Market Liquidity and Funding Liquidity* (2009).
- Valid use: monitoring whether funding terms are tightening or easing.
- Invalid use: claiming a universal market-stress predictor, a structural
  morphology, or a complete causal explanation.

## Market constraint pressure (`D` compatibility key)

- Product name: **Market constraint pressure**.
- Observable: standardized tightening in risk-bearing and market functioning,
  using NFCI risk, MOVE, and the SPX rolling-spread proxy.
- Mechanism lineage: dealer/intermediary risk-bearing capacity and the price of
  balance sheet constrain market liquidity; reserve and funding conditions are
  interpreted through established intermediary and reserve-demand literature.
- Valid use: monitoring whether market action space is becoming more constrained.
- Invalid use: treating the gauge as a literal geometric degree of freedom or
  as proof of the archived Deformation state equations.

## Shared measurement discipline

- Inputs must come from an admitted Harvester release.
- Transformations are causal rolling standardizations; no future values enter.
- Missing components lower the claim ceiling and remain visible.
- Gauge values may be used by research and bounded monitoring only.
- K, X, absorption capacity, and non-commutativity are not part of this product.
