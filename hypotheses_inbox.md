# Hypotheses inbox

Two rules only:

1. Entry has zero threshold: one line is enough; no format, justification or self-censorship is required.
2. Inbox content has zero authority: it cannot enter a decision, count as a claim, or be read by a judgment/selection agent without passing the existing research and promotion gates.

The generator and selector must be different sessions. Once a week, the
generator samples one Paper case card and one current instrument reading and
adds five deliberately loose “what if” questions. The session stops after 30
minutes and must not score, promote or adjudicate its own output.
