# 1998 LTCM - Leverage, Liquidity, And Forced Convergence Unwind

## 1. Event Summary

LTCM's convergence trades became unstable after global spread widening and
liquidity withdrawal, leading to coordinated private-sector intervention in 1998.

## 2. Structural Diagnosis

- `M`: high, convergence anchors failed under stress
- `D`: collapsing, liquidation paths narrowed
- `K`: high, similar shocks propagated nonlinearly
- `X`: high, leverage and crowded positions stored pressure

## 3. Anchor Mismatch

Model-implied convergence anchors diverged from liquidation and funding anchors.

## 4. Path Feasibility

Crowded trades reduced exit paths; forced sales threatened to move prices against
remaining positions.

## 5. Shadow Accumulation

Leverage and counterparty exposure accumulated before public realization.

## 6. Trigger Vs Potential

- Trigger: spread widening and liquidity withdrawal after external shocks.
- Stored potential: leveraged convergence positions and crowded exits.

## 7. Data Candidates

- public case studies and official reports

## 8. Relevant Benchmarks

- liquidity benchmarks

## 9. What This Case Supports

## 10. What It Does Not Prove
