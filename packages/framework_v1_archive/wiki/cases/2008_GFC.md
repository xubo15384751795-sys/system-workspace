# 2008 GFC - Shadow Accumulation And Funding Anchor Break

## 1. Event Summary

The 2007-2009 global financial crisis involved mortgage-credit losses, funding
stress, shadow-banking fragility, and forced deleveraging.

## 2. Structural Diagnosis

- `M`: high, valuation and funding anchors diverged
- `D`: collapsing, funding and liquidation paths contracted
- `K`: high, transitions became discontinuous across regimes
- `X`: high, shadow exposures accumulated before realization

## 3. Anchor Mismatch

Structured-credit valuation anchors diverged from funding and liquidation anchors.

## 4. Path Feasibility

Repo, securitization, and dealer-intermediation paths contracted under stress.

## 5. Shadow Accumulation

Off-balance-sheet, structured-credit, and maturity-transformation pressures built
before forced realization.

## 6. Trigger Vs Potential

- Trigger: mortgage-credit losses, funding runs, and counterparty stress.
- Stored potential: shadow-banking leverage, maturity mismatch, opaque exposure.
