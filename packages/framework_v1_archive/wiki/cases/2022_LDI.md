# 2022 LDI - Rates Shock, Collateral Calls, And Path Contraction

## 1. Event Summary

In September and October 2022, UK gilt yields moved sharply, pressuring LDI
strategies through collateral calls and liquidation needs.

## 2. Structural Diagnosis

- `M`: high, liability, collateral, and market anchors diverged

- `K`: high, rates transition became nonlinear

## 3. Anchor Mismatch

Liability-hedging anchors diverged from collateral and liquidation anchors during the gilt shock.

## 4. Path Feasibility
